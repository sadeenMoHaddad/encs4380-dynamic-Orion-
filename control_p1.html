Pick a plant from Homework 1, set the gains and a setpoint step, and watch what each term does. P makes the loop faster but leaves an error, I removes the error, and D calms the overshoot.

Try it: start with P only (Ki = Kd = 0), then add I, then D, and watch the four numbers above the table. The derivative acts on the measurement, filtered over 10 ms, so a setpoint step gives no "derivative kick". The loop is simulated with RK4, step 1 ms (see control_lib.js).
